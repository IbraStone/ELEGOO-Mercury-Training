Welcome and safety

Resin printing makes detailed models, but the liquid resin and the washing alcohol need respect. Learn the rules first.

  • Wear a mask and gloves whenever you handle photopolymer resin. Avoid direct skin contact.
  • Keep the printer, resin and accessories out of the reach of children.
  • Use the printer indoors, dry, out of direct sunlight and dust. Aim for 25–30 °C and ventilate the room for heat and resin odour.
  • Fill the resin tank to at least 1/3 of its volume, never above the MAX line.
  • Wash models with 95% (or higher) ethyl alcohol or isopropyl alcohol, unless you use water-washable resin.
  • In an emergency, turn off the power supply first.
  • Do not take the lid off the Mercury Plus while it is curing. The UV lights are hazardous.
  • Do not disassemble or modify the machines yourself, or the warranty ends.

Setup and self-check

On every startup the Mars 5 Ultra checks its own hardware.

  1. Place the printer on a stable surface, attach the WiFi antenna and the AI camera, and connect power.
  2. Switch on. Do not touch the printer during the self-check.
  3. Read the list: LCD connection, Z and X motors, LED light source, fan, LED temperature and mechanical sensor. A red mark means a fault to fix; press Refresh to check again.
  4. Find the release film counter on the self-check page (Tool, then Device self-test). At about 60,000 uses the printer asks you to replace the release film. After replacing it, press Refresh to reset the counter.
Tip. A loose or whitened release film causes layer breakage and failed prints. Replace it as soon as the machine prompts.

Your first print

Prepare

  1. Insert the build plate into the connecting block and press the handle down. The plate self-levels, so no manual levelling is needed.
  2. Pour resin slowly into the tank, between 1/3 and the MAX line.
  3. Close the anti-UV cover.
  4. Choose the sliced test model on the touch screen (from USB or the network) and start. The machine self-checks first, so do not touch it.

After printing

  1. Slide the drip tray in parallel to the resin tank and tighten the screw knobs on both sides. It stops resin dripping onto the machine.
  2. Lift the handle and remove the build plate carefully, because the LCD screen can be damaged.
  3. Remove the model with the scraper, or wash it on the plate (next modules).
Caution. If a print fails, clear cured bits from the tank, and filter the resin through the funnel when pouring it back. Leftover residue can damage the LCD screen on the next print.

Resin calibration

Find the best exposure time for a new resin in one test print.

  1. On the printer open Tool, then Resin calibration, and choose 4, 6 or 8 sections. The screen is split into equal zones.
  2. Import a sliced model using the + button. Keep it off the section boundaries.
  3. Set an exposure time for each zone starting at Zone 1. Times must decrease or stay equal across zones (the manual example uses 2.80, 2.50, 2.30 and 1.80 s).
  4. Press Start, then judge the results and use the best zone’s time as your resin’s exposure.
Tip. Test each new resin and colour. Bottom exposure and exposure time depend on the resin you use.

Wash with the Mercury Plus

Never place a model loose in the bucket. Keep it on the build plate or in the basket.

  1. Plug in and switch on. Press MODE until the water-drop icon (wash mode) shows.
  2. Remove the curing turntable. Add washing liquid to the bucket below the maximum line, then seat the bucket in its slot.
  3. Models ≤ 15 mm, still on the build plate: fix the plate to the adjustable bracket (max model height 120 mm) and hang it on top of the pillar.
  4. Models larger than 15 mm, removed from the plate: put them in the basket (max height 160 mm) and hang it on the pillar.
  5. Close the anti-UV lid. Press TIME+ or TIME− (30 s per press, up to 30 minutes), then ON/OFF.
  6. It beeps when done. Remove the model and air dry it. Seal the bucket with its lid. Washing liquid can be reused.
Caution. Wipe up spilled liquid at once so it can’t run into the machine and cause a short circuit.

Cure with the Mercury Plus

  1. Press MODE until the light-bulb icon (cure mode) shows.
  2. Remove the cleaning bucket. Seat the curing turntable on the centre positioning hole.
  3. Place the washed and dried model in the middle of the turntable and close the lid.
  4. Set the time with TIME+ or TIME−, then press ON/OFF. A slight jitter is normal.
  5. It beeps when finished.

Maintenance and firmware

Mars 5 Ultra

  • Never scrape the tank with sharp tools. It damages the release film.
  • Clean the tank before switching resin colour. Wipe the build plate before and after printing.
  • Check the machine’s exterior and moving parts before each print.
  • If the Z axis squeaks, lubricate the lead screw. Check at least every 2–3 months, more often with heavy use.
  • If unused for 48 hours, filter the resin and pour it back into its sealed bottle.
  • The LCD lasts about 2000+ hours. Unplug after printing and keep the screen clean.

Mercury Plus

  • After about 30 reuses, let the washing liquid stand around 10 hours, pour the clear liquid off the top into another container, and clean the settled waste from the bucket.
  • If the bottom rotor sticks, remove it for cleaning (one screw). If it rattles, retighten the screw.

Firmware upgrade

  • Local: copy the firmware file to USB, then Settings, Version Upgrade, Local Upgrade, pick the version and Confirm.
  • Network: when online, a pop-up offers printer or camera firmware updates. Choose Upgrade now.
